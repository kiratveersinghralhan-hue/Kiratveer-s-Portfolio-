01 / CONTEXT

Useful business AI needs more than a conversation. It needs the right context, clear permissions and a reliable distinction between a suggestion and an action.

02 / THE IDEA

Build an operating layer where a business can keep its knowledge, ask for help and review proposed changes before they happen.

An actively developed software product bringing business knowledge, AI assistance and human approval into one organizational workspace.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

A Command Center connects to Business Memory, an Approval Center and workspace integrations. The interface gives knowledge, decisions and actions their own clear place.

04 / THE BUILD
  • Organizational workspaces with authenticated identity and role-based access.
  • Business Memory for organizational facts, preferences, policies, processes and context.
  • AI assistance through registered tools, with permission checks and separate review and execution steps.
  • Google account integration and explicit, bounded read-only Gmail previews are being developed.
  • Application and database work are supported by automated tests. Development and review are ongoing.
