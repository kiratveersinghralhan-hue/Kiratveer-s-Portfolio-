LinkedIn Launch Post — TripMitra AI Studio

I’m excited to share one of my main projects: TripMitra AI Studio.

TripMitra AI Studio is an AI-powered trip planning web app built to solve a common problem: group trip planning gets messy very fast.

Instead of handling everything through scattered chats, TripMitra helps groups create one smart trip website with:
• AI-generated trip plans
• Group voting
• RSVP and food preferences
• Live fund and expense tracking
• Budget-based planning
• Final day-wise trip plan
• Admin dashboard and notifications
• Razorpay payment integration
• Affiliate/referral program

This project helped me learn how to think beyond just design and code. I worked on product flow, user experience, payments, backend logic, SEO, analytics, admin tools and launch planning.

Live project: https://tripmitra.store

I’m still improving it, but I’m proud that it is a real product, not just a demo.
